## To be covered (from the architecture brief v1.0)

- Goals from the public challenge summary and where the app addresses them (brief §15):
  - What is changing? Variable, unit and description in the catalog.
  - Where is it changing? Coordinates and their honest spatial meaning; region means later.
  - How does it change over time? Annual series, selectable period, fixed baseline.
  - How strong is the change? Trend per decade with the period actually used.
  - Is the trend statistically clear? Interval, method, sample size, quality status and limits.
- For each goal: the screen and the tests that cover it.
- Verification status: the full challenge page could not be retrieved reliably on 30 Oct 2026; full requirements, judging and submission rules are not verified yet. Record any differences from the baseline and the resulting changes.
